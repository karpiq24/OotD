The interaction with the silver dragon **Nepheli** (the mother of Ventus) in the Tower of Praxis reveals a creature deeply traumatized by her captivity under Sidon. In this campaign's interpretation, the five "Cydonites" (the party disguised as guards) find her bound in a magic-suppressing circle by adamantine chains.

Below is the dialogue tree and the key narrative talking points to use for your own *Odyssey of the Dragonlords* session.

---

## 🐲 Nepheli Dialogue Tree

### **1. Initial Contact (The Rescue)**

* 
**Party:** "We were sent here to ensure this area is not compromised." 


* 
**Nepheli:** (Imploringly) "..." (She makes a face of distress, seeking help).


* 
**Party:** (Revealing identity) "Is that my little girl? [Ventus] ... We've come to stop Sidon and save you." 


* 
**Nepheli:** "I stopped working for him a long time ago. Good luck with that. I am bound by adamantine and wards. My keeper is **Galaran**; he wears the silver key and knows the command words." 



### **2. The Plan (Shape-shifting)**

* 
**Party:** "Can you change size to get out of the chains?" 


* 
**Nepheli:** "I could change my shape, but not while within this circle. Adamantine is immutable; the chains won't change with me." 


* 
**Action:** Once the party dispels the magic circle, she shifts into a human form to slip out of the shackles.



### **3. Post-Release (The "Feral" State)**

* 
**Nepheli:** (Immediately begins eating the remains of a fallen Minotaur guard) "..." (Tears at flesh with draconic strength, appearing "blood-stupid" or feral).


* 
**Party:** "Are you related to Pythor?" 


* 
**Nepheli:** (In a blood stupor) "Pythor’s my father? What’s a Pythor?" 


* 
**Nepheli:** "I will accompany you until such a time as I can leave this tower. I don't plan to do any killing, but I want revenge." 



### **4. The Harem & Eggs (Lore Dump)**

* 
**Nepheli:** "If you need to hide, go to my harem. You'd have to slay them all, but they have treasure." 


* 
**Party:** "What’s with the eggs?" 


* 
**Nepheli:** "I lay eggs all the time. My mates are silver, copper, and bronze. They are relatively new; I killed my previous mates when I tried to escape." 


* 
**Nepheli:** "My eggs were always taken by Sidon. Those mates... they are intensely cruel and savage."

## 🎭 Key Talking Points & Personality Notes

If you are running this encounter, keep these thematic elements in mind to mirror the vibe of the transcript:

* **Feral Hunger:** After decades of imprisonment and forced breeding, Nepheli is not a "noble" dragon. She is hungry and ruthless. She eats the Minotaur guards immediately upon release.


* 
**The "Harem" Conflict:** Sidon has forced her to mate with other metallic dragons (Silver, Copper, Bronze) whose eggs are then stolen. These mates are "young bucks" who have become "intensely cruel" due to their environment/corruption, and Nepheli has no love for them.


* 
**Contradictory Goals:** She initially claims she doesn't want to kill anymore, yet she is driven by a desire for "revenge against those who abused [her]" and eventually charges her breath weapon when the party tries to give her "unsolicited advice" on relaxing.


* 
**Physical Trauma:** Her "human stomach" is described as distended or altered because she is "pregnant 24/7" to provide dragons for Sidon’s army.
