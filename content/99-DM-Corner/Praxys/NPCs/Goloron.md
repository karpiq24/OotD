This session features a dramatic confrontation with **Galaron**, the Titanborn son of Lutheria and Sidon, in the "Arena of Death." Galaron is portrayed as a cold, arrogant, and somewhat "video-gamey" antagonist who views the players as mere entertainment for his court.

Below is the dialogue tree and the key talking points you can use to roleplay him in your campaign.

---

## Galaron Dialogue Tree

### Phase 1: The Introduction (Arrogance)

*When the party first enters the arena and is spotted by Galaron on his throne.*

* 
**Galaron:** "Hmm... you have caused quite a stir, haven't you? Don't worry. You can make it up to me by entertaining me in the arena!" 


* 
**The Proclamation:** "Let the match begin!" 



### Phase 2: During the Fight (Taunting)

*Use these lines as the party struggles with the arena traps (the Iron Ball, Pit Traps, etc.).*

* **On the Pit Trap:** "Wait, my pit trap did work? And now it didn't? How is this possible?!" (If a player bypasses a trap) 


* 
**On the Iron Ball:** "I love that ball! Now, now... next time for sure!" 


* 
**On His Invention:** "Who invented the balls? This guy! That's right! Told you they’re good!" 



### Phase 3: The Confrontation (Personal/Family Stakes)

*When the dragons are dead and the party engages him directly.*

* 
**Galaron (Enraged):** "You killed my father’s dragons! I will make you suffer for killing my pets!" 


* 
**To a Fellow Titanborn/Chosen (Cyrus):** "I know you... you’re my mother’s lapdog. Her finest creation. Why do you stand against my family when you are a part of it?" 


* 
*If questioned about his loyalty:* "Each of my parents bears a great burden. I do what I can to lighten the load. Your death will lighten it even further." 





### Phase 4: The End (Defeat)

*His final words as he falls.*

* 
**Galaron:** "Mother... Father... I have failed you..."

## Key Talking Points for Galaron

If you are running this encounter, keep these thematic pillars in mind to stay true to this version of the character:

* **The "Showman" Persona:** Galaron views the struggle of the "Chosen" as a spectator sport. He claps, cheers for his traps, and plays to the crowd of goatlings and minotaurs.


* **Deep Family Loyalty:** Unlike some of his siblings, Galaron is a zealot for his father’s (Sidon) plans. He believes the world is "too stupid and weak" to manage itself and that his family must unify everyone through unconditional worship.


* **Arrogant Artificer:** He is obsessed with the mechanics of his tower. He takes personal pride in his "adamantine" traps and "bullet storm" cannons.


* 
**Necromantic Ties:** As the son of Lutheria, he uses the "Grim Harvest" to devour the souls of fallen allies (and enemies) to restore his own health during the fight.
