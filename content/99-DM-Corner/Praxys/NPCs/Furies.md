## 🗝️ Key Talking Points for the GM

If you are running the Furies, use these points to flavor their dialogue:

* 
**The Rules Manifested:** Emphasize that the Furies are not "people" in the traditional sense; they are the rules of the universe made flesh. They enforce the "Law of Thylea," not necessarily Sidon’s personal whims.


* **The Mother Goddess:** They are loyal to **Thylea**. They view the current Titan rule (Sidon and Lutheria) as a distortion of what Thylea intended for the world.


* **Disdain for the Titans:** They find Sidon's arrogance unearned. He failed to maintain his divine gifts (like the orrery) and relies on cruelty rather than the sanctity of his word.


* 
**Mystical Awareness:** They can sense if the players are "Breaking the Wheel" or acting as "Anomalies" that Thylea has produced to reset the balance of the world.


* 
**The Secret of the Tower Eye:** The "Eye of Sidon" is actually a kidnapped celestial being (a child of the Nix/stars), which explains why it is so powerful and why the Titans are "allergic" to it.
