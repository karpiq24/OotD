## Chef Ramses Dialog Tree

This tree follows the party's interaction from their initial entry to the formation of "Operation Bread Bowl."

### **1. The Initial Confrontation (The "Entry" Branch)**

* 
**Ramses:** "Just what the bloody hell do you want? Why are you in my kitchen? Why are you here?!" 


* **User Response: "We're here to place an order."**
* 
**Ramses:** "You can't place an order in my kitchen! You're low-ranking mediums; you shouldn't even be on this floor!" 


* **User Response: (Attempts to flatter/perform)**
* **Ramses (to his staff):** "Step out in the hallway! Step out!" (To the players): "You want a custom order? I want a god damn break! Thousands of people in this tower and I’m one man with some goatlings!" 



### **2. The "Galar-on" Deception (The "Sidi-Son" Branch)**

* 
**Ramses:** "What are you trying to order? A Hero’s Feast for tomorrow?" 


* **User Response: "Galar-on (Sidon’s son) sent us. It’s for his private dinner."**
* 
**Ramses:** "So, the boy is trying to get extras on the side? Probably trying to impress that nymph girlfriend or those hoity-toities from Elysium." 




* **User Response: "He gave us a lot of money to compensate you."**
* 
**Ramses:** "Do I look like a man who needs money? Do you know how many five-star restaurants I own around Thylea?" 



### **3. The Reveal (The "Hero" Branch)**

* 
**Ramses:** "You're just a cytokinite... just a little soldier." 


* **User Response: (Reveals true identity/strength)**
* 
**Ramses (investigating/touching armor):** "Well... you're the bloody heroes of the Oracle, aren't you? What are you doing here?" 




* **User Response: "We want to stop the war."**
* 
**Ramses (tearing up):** "I’ve been thinking about all the people that are going to die. The world doesn't have to be such an ugly place. What if Sidon was stopped today?" 


### **4. The Pitch: Operation Bread Bowl**

* 
**Ramses:** "Sidon has true sight, he’d see right through your disguise. But you know what true sight can't see through? **Bread.**" 


* 
**The Plan:** "I’m going to put you guys into hollowed-out loaves of bread and send you up with the meal. Once you're there, you jump out and kill Sidon." 


* **User Response: "Can we poison the food with the metal he's allergic to?"**
* 
**Ramses:** "I take my cooking seriously! If word got out I served poison... I don't know if I could bounce back." 




* **The Closer: "We have a job for you in Estoria/Hexonia."**
* 
**Ramses:** "Bringing refined cooking to a po-dunk country bumpkin town... it’s a bold new world I want to be a part of."

## Key Talking Points & Persona Notes

When roleplaying Chef Ramses, use these specific character beats:

* 
**The "Culinancy" Code:** He refuses to use "regular" ingredients for Sidon; he uses the best of the best, even if it means betraying Sidon's orders to save the ingredients for the "Family Dinner".


* 
**The Burden of Leadership:** He is exhausted by the scale of the war effort, feeding thousands with only "lawful goatlings" as staff.


* **Signature Insults:**
* "This fish is so raw he's still finding Nemo!" 


* "This cheesy flatbread is so distinct if you took it to Italy you'd get arrested!" 


* **Emotional Vulnerability:** Despite his screaming, he is deeply saddened by the impending loss of life in the war. He and his chicken familiar, **Cock n' Balls**, share synchronized crying fits when discussing peace.


* 
**Mechanical Requirements:** * The "Bread Bowl" mission requires the players to return by **4:30 PM** for the evening meal.


* He can provide a **Hero's Feast** that takes 10 minutes to bake and 1 hour to eat.
