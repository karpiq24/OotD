A tense parley on a staircase inside a dark timber fort built into a mossy hill, a wide staircase with waist-high wooden steps spiralling around the trunk of a giant ancient oak, narrow arrow-slit windows, smoke and scorched wooden walls. A faint, glassy, shimmering wall of force blocks the stairs. Below it, a war band of centaurs with raised spears and bows fills the lower landing; above it, a soaking-wet minotaur and a young satyr address them.

He wears a draped white toga with a gold Greek-key border and embroidered green hop vines, the fabric stretched over his belly. A cheerful, self-satisfied grin. The aesthetic is a Greco-Roman brewer and mythological creature.
