A brutal, dramatic moment of victory in a battle inside a dark timber fort built into a mossy hill, a wide staircase with waist-high wooden steps spiralling around the trunk of a giant ancient oak, narrow arrow-slit windows, smoke and scorched wooden walls. At the top of the spiral staircase a huge minotaur stands at the edge of the landing, holding up by the horns the severed head of a black-furred enemy minotaur chieftain and bellowing down the stairwell for everyone to go home; the head is about to be tossed down the steps. Below him, on the steps, the defenders falter: two centaur warriors turn to flee down the stairs, while crude, half-finished giants with uneven grey skin and mismatched limbs carrying long pikes, and hunched, emaciated giant hag witches hung with skulls and fangs spitting thick green phlegm still push upward through drifting fog.

At the top of the stairs, roaring, holding the severed head aloft in one hand and a great axe in the other, splattered with blood and green venom: very tall, towering, heavyset middle-aged male minotaur, a head taller than other minotaurs, still broad-shouldered with strong arms, but with a big round beer belly, brown fur. His bovine head has long curved pale grey horns wrapped with gold Greek-key bands, bright blue eyes, a long bovine bull's head with a long, broad muzzle and large flared nostrils (a true bull's face) and a gold ring through the nose. He has a long thick blonde mane swept back and a full blonde beard braided at the chin and tied with a gold bead, and a wreath of green hop leaves and hop cones between his horns. He wears a draped white toga with a gold Greek-key border and embroidered green hop vines, the fabric stretched over his belly. A cheerful, self-satisfied grin. The aesthetic is a Greco-Roman brewer and mythological creature.

Behind him on the landing lie the bodies of a slain giant minotaur warlord and a nymph; a bronze dragon crouches near the wall with lightning crackling in its jaws. Warm torchlight from above, cold grey fog below, dust and sparks in the air. Dark Fantasy Oil Painting, heavy brushwork, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, high detail.
