A haunting, lonely fantasy scene inside an impossible labyrinth. A lone warrior stands at the intersection of endless identical stone corridors that fold into each other at wrong angles — staircases running sideways and upside down, doorways opening onto more of the same hallway, an Escher-like prison with no exit. He has stopped walking. He is speaking aloud to no one, chin raised, eyes closed, defiant and calm.

A large, jagged scar runs along the side of his neck and jaw. He wears an ornate breastplate. He wears a large, magical gauntlet on one hand.
