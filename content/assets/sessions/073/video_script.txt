## Clip 1: Orion finding picture of Lutheria feet

### Step 1: First Frame (Static Image)
*Model Suggestion: Nano Banana 2*
**Prompt (Copy this)**:
> In a colossal, opulent ancient Greek-style bedroom illuminated by dancing candlelight, a male fantasy warrior with an athletic build is kneeling on the pristine marble floor next to a massive, luxurious king-sized bed covered in thick, exotic animal furs. He wears highly ornate golden plate armor with intricate filigree in a Greco-Roman spartan style. He dramatically grabs the thick linen cloth covering it and pulls it away, revealing an incredibly detailed, beautifully painted canvas of a pale woman's bare feet. Dust motes swirl in the air illuminated by nearby candlelight.
