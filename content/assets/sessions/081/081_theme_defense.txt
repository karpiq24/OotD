A sweeping, chaotic cinematic panorama of a besieged ancient Greek metropolis of white marble and terracotta rooftops, whole districts ablaze, columns toppled and streets choked with rubble and smoke. Desperate urban warfare rages across multiple fronts. Ranks of bronze clockwork automatons, gleaming and faceless, grind forward against defenders. Fierce fur-clad amazon warrior-women loose arrows and swing axes on one flank; stout armored dwarves in heavy plate lock shields on another; disciplined human legionnaires in crested Corinthian helmets and red-trimmed armor hold a line before a grand palace. Towering stone giants smash through buildings while monstrous fish-like merrow warriors scramble up from the harbor.

Above it all, a colossal humanoid war-construct — a gargantuan animated bronze-and-marble statue of a heroic god wielding an enormous spear — strides through the ruined streets, dwarfing the buildings. In the smoke-filled sky, majestic dragons wheel and a floating fortress hangs among storm clouds.

Ash falling like snow, embers drifting, fires reflecting off marble, shafts of harsh light breaking through smoke. High quality digital fantasy art, dramatic cinematic lighting, vast epic scale, highly detailed.
