A terrifying, surreal nightmare feast in a ruined ancient slave market of rough stone, under a thick, unnatural, luminous multicolored fog. Half-naked maddened citizens covered in wounds and human bite-marks lurch in jerky puppet-like motions, some frozen mid-transformation with human faces on swine bodies and delicate hands sprouting from goat flanks, cheering deliriously. Pigs wearing tattered silk robes scurry between rusted iron cages. Roasting spits drip greasy meat of suspicious origin over campfires, and a deranged citizen holds out a hot chunk of roast in his hand. The atmosphere is a blend of a macabre, deranged carnival and a dark fantasy horror scene.

Eerie colored light, drifting fog, embers rising from the fires, chaotic and unsettling dreamlike mood. High quality digital fantasy art, dark fantasy, dramatic cinematic lighting, highly detailed.
