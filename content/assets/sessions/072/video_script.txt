# Video Script: Sesja 72 - Śniadanie u Tytana

**Art Style Set**: Arcane (League of Legends TV series style), painterly textures, sharp angles, expressive lighting, cinematic depth of field, vibrant colors, Fortiche production style.

**Global Ingredients** (Składniki): 
- **Sydon**: Potężny, umięśniony bóg o białych włosach, czerwonych oczach i trzecim oku na czole (@Sydon).
- **Orestes**: Minotaur w białej todze z furiackim spojrzeniem (@Orestes).
- **Gigantyczny Bochenek**: Gargantuiczny chleb na złotym półmisku.

## Clip 1: Cisza przed Burzą

### Step 1: First Frame (Static Image)
*Model Suggestion: Nano Banana 2*
**Prompt (Copy this)**:
> A cinematic close-up of a gargantuan, golden-brown rustic loaf of bread resting on a massive, ornate golden platter. The bread's crust is dusted with flour and has thick, scored patterns. The platter sits on a colossal obsidian table in an opulent throne room. In the background, seven massive, gold-dripping thrones with blood-red upholstery are arranged in a semicircle. The walls are adorned with magnificent purple banners with golden embroidery. Near the table, a gigantic, realistic, convex glowing blue topographical map of Thylea is visible on the floor. Golden sunbeams cut through ozone-filled air. High quality Arcane League of Legends TV series style, painterly textures, sharp angles, expressive lighting, cinematic depth of field, vibrant colors, Fortiche production style.

### Step 2: Base Video (Action & Audio)
*Model: Veo 3.1 - Fast | Tool: Set Video 'Rozpocznij' frame using image from Step 1*
*Camera Suggestion: Najazd kamerą (Zoom In) / Slow Dolly In*
**Action Prompt (Copy this)**:
> The camera slowly dollies in towards the giant loaf of bread. Tense silence. Suddenly, the bread crust subtly shifts and vibrates from within. A small crumb falls off the side. The air around the bread seems to crackle with faint magical static. The lighting flickers slightly as if responding to a contained power.

**Audio & Dialogue Prompt (Optional)**:
> [Background sounds: Niskie, rytmiczne drżenie. Dźwięk pękającej chrupiącej skórki chleba. Ciche sapanie i szept "Teraz..."]

## Clip 2: Eksplozja Bochenka

### Step 1: First Frame (Static Image)
*Model Suggestion: Nano Banana 2*
**Prompt (Copy this)**:
> A side view of a colossal, immensely muscular elderly male god with tanned skin and wild flowing white hair and beard. He wears a draped white toga, thick gold bicep armbands, and a wide gold belt. He has glowing red eyes and a glowing red third eye on his forehead. He is reaching a massive, weathered hand toward a gargantuan loaf of bread on a golden platter. The scene is set in his opulent throne room, with seven gold-dripping thrones and purple banners in the background. A glowing topographical map on the floor illuminates the scene with a cold blue light. High quality Arcane League of Legends TV series style, painterly textures, sharp angles, expressive lighting, cinematic depth of field, vibrant colors, Fortiche production style.

### Step 2: Base Video (Action & Audio)
*Model: Veo 3.1 - Fast | Tool: Set Video 'Rozpocznij' frame using image from Step 1*
*Camera Suggestion: Wyjazd kamerą (Zoom Out) / Dynamic Tilt Up*
**Action Prompt (Copy this)**:
> As the god's hand touches the crust, the bread suddenly explodes from the center in a shower of crumbs and golden light. A powerful male minotaur with brown fur, large curved grey horns, and piercing glowing blue eyes bursts out from the heart of the loaf. He is mid-leap, his face contorted in a furiack shout, swinging a massive battleaxe downward. Behind him, hints of other heroes are visible emerging from the debris. The god's expression shifts from surprise to shock, his glowing eyes widening.

> 2. lean human wizard with messy dark brown hair, a full beard, and two bronze star emblems on his chest, casting a blue magical orb.
