## Clip 3: Ścieżka Głębin (Prawe Oko)

### Step 1: First Frame (Static Image)
*Model Suggestion: Nano Banana 2*
*Ingredient: Attach @Versir*
**Prompt (Copy this)**:
> Wide angle shot looking downward into a vast, dark ocean abyss. Deep underwater, hundreds of meters below the surface. The scene is dominated by the enormous silhouette of a gargantuan chained titan filling the lower half of the frame — an ancient colossus with impossibly broad shoulders, weathered stone-like skin, kneeling and bent forward under the immense weight of tower foundations pressing down from above. Thick glowing adamantine spikes and heavy chains are driven into the titan's shoulders and anchored into the rocky seabed. In the upper portion of the frame, a small figure swims downward toward the titan — an adult male with a slender build, pale alabaster skin, short blonde messy quiff hair, piercing cyan eyes glowing faintly in the dark water, a jagged scar on his neck and jaw, wearing an ornate breastplate, one hand a stump, the other wearing a large magical gauntlet crackling with faint cyan energy. His hair drifts in the current. Faint bioluminescent particles float in the water. The only light sources are the man's glowing cyan eyes, the faint runes on the adamantine restraints, and the distant surface light far above. The titan is massive compared to the man — at least fifty times his size. No land or tower visible, only deep ocean. High quality digital fantasy art.

### Step 2: Base Video (Action & Audio)
*Model: Veo 3.1 - Fast | Tool: Set Video 'Rozpocznij' frame using image from Step 1*
*Camera Suggestion: Najazd kamerą (Dolly Forward/Down) following the man downward toward the titan*
**Action Prompt (Copy this)**:
> Cinematic underwater shot following a pale-skinned man in ornate armor swimming powerfully downward through dark ocean depths. The camera tracks behind and slightly above him. As he descends, the colossal shape of a chained titan becomes clearer — massive shoulders, ancient weathered skin, thick adamantine spikes piercing its body. The man reaches one of the glowing adamantine spikes and raises his magical gauntlet. The gauntlet crackles with bright cyan energy. He swings his fist into the spike with all his strength. On impact, a shockwave ripples through the water, and cracks spider-web across the adamantine surface. The titan's eyes open — two ancient, golden eyes reflecting the pale man's glowing silhouette. 8 second continuous shot.

**Audio & Dialogue Prompt**:
> Background sounds: Muffled, oppressive deep-water pressure. A distant, low whale-like groan from the chained titan — ancient and sorrowful. The crackle of magical energy building in the gauntlet, muffled by water. The impact: a deep, resonating underwater boom that sends a pressure wave through the scene. Then, silence — broken only by a single, earth-shaking heartbeat from the titan.

### Step 3: Expand / Rozszerz
*Use when in the video player/editor after generating Step 2*
**Action Continuation Prompt (Co dalej?)**:
> More cracks spread across the adamantine spike. The titan shifts — for the first time in centuries, one massive shoulder rises slightly. Chains groan and snap taut. Debris cascades from the tower foundations above, falling through the water in slow motion. The pale man floats back from the impact, looking up at the titan's now-open golden eyes. A second heartbeat pounds through the water, stronger than the first. The darkness around the titan begins to recede as golden light emanates from within the ancient colossus.
