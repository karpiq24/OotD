# Video Script: Sesja 71

**Art Style**: High quality digital fantasy art

## Scene 1: Rozmowa z Paradoksem na Statku
**Start Frame Prompt**: 
A majestic bronze dragon with shiny metallic scales resting on the deck of a wooden ship. Warm evening lighting. In the background a powerful male minotaur with a muscular build, broad shoulders, and brown fur. His bovine head features large, curved dark grey horns, piercing glowing blue eyes, and a bull snout. He has stylish, thick blonde hair swept back and a well-groomed blonde fur beard. He is dressed in a draped white toga with an ornate red sash featuring carved patterns and a textured grey shoulder strap. High quality digital fantasy art.
**Video Prompt**: 
Cinematic medium shot of a majestic bronze dragon with shiny metallic scales resting on the deck of a wooden ship, looking with extreme disgust. He snorts visibly. The camera tracks back slowly to reveal a powerful male minotaur with a muscular build, brown fur, large curved dark grey horns, thick blonde hair, holding a wooden mug of beer, smiling goofily and swaying awkwardly back and forth. Warm evening lighting. High quality digital fantasy art.

## Scene 2: Podwodny Tytan pod Wieżą
**Start Frame Prompt**: 
A colossal dark underwater scene where the massive foundation pillars of a stone tower disappear into the deep abyss. Faint glowing magical runes trace the stone. Dim light filters from the surface far above. High quality digital fantasy art.
**Video Prompt**: 
Cinematic pan down the massive foundation pillars of a stone tower deep underwater, revealing a gargantuan underwater titan with incredibly broad shoulders holding up the foundation. The titan strains and pushes against the structure, shaking slightly. He is chained by thick adamantine spikes driven into the seabed. Dark, deep-sea aesthetic, glowing magical runes pulse rhythmically. High quality digital fantasy art.

## Scene 3: Przeprawa i Cyklop z Mopem
**Start Frame Prompt**: 
A massive marble latrine room in a dark fantasy dungeon. The marble floors are dirty. A closed wooden stall door stands partially ajar. Dramatic dark shadows cast by dim torchlight. High quality digital fantasy art.
**Video Prompt**: 
Cinematic tracking shot in a massive marble latrine room. A gigantic deformed cyclops who surprisingly has two eyes instead of one walks forward carrying a large wooden mop and a bucket in his hands. He walks to the center of the room, looks left towards a closed wooden stall with a confused and disgusted expression, and then hastily turns around to walk out of the room, looking frightened. High quality digital fantasy art.

## Scene 4: Ściana płomieni i Owady
**Start Frame Prompt**: 
A dark dungeon hallway made of massive heavy stone. Shadows dominate the scene. Through the darkness, faint scuttling sounds can be assumed. High quality digital fantasy art.
**Video Prompt**: 
Cinematic wide shot deep inside a dark dungeon hallway. A young adult male human wizard with intense blue-grey eyes, a strong jawline, short messy dark brown hair, and a full dark brown beard, wearing a rust-brown hooded tunic, steps forward aggressively and thrusts both hands forward. Suddenly, a massive, raging wall of deep red fire bursts upwards from the floor to the ceiling in front of him. In the intense flames, terrifying giant insectoid monsters with segmented dark-red exoskeletons are caught, burning and screeching. Intense action, dramatic warm lighting from the wall of fire. High quality digital fantasy art.
