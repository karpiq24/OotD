The top-floor chamber of a timber hill fort, full of chests, barrels and heaped treasure, a straw bed with silk quilts. A door has been torn from its hinges.

1. On the silk-covered straw bed, lying still and lifeless: a slender water nymph with pale blue-green skin, long dark wet hair and a thin silken shift.
2. Standing over the bed, holding the torn-off door in both hands like a shield, looking toward the side door: very tall, towering, heavyset middle-aged male minotaur, a head taller than other minotaurs, still broad-shouldered with strong arms, but with a big round beer belly, brown fur. His bovine head has long curved pale grey horns wrapped with gold Greek-key bands, bright blue eyes, a long bovine bull's head with a long, broad muzzle and large flared nostrils (a true bull's face) and a gold ring through the nose. He has a long thick blonde mane swept back and a full blonde beard braided at the chin and tied with a gold bead, and a wreath of green hop leaves and hop cones between his horns. He wears a draped white toga with a gold Greek-key border and embroidered green hop vines, the fabric stretched over his belly. A cheerful, self-satisfied grin. The aesthetic is a Greco-Roman brewer and mythological creature.
3. Bursting in through a side door on the left, mid-charge with his horns lowered, eyes blazing with golden light, his grief turning to rage: a huge, towering male minotaur warlord with a muscular build, dark brown fur scarred from many battles, long thick black horns, a bull snout flaring with rage and eyes that blaze with a golden light. He wears heavy ornate dark bronze armor and wields a gleaming silver-white mithral longsword.

On the right, a glowing ancient amphora with golden light seeping from its mouth stands among the treasures. Dramatic contrast of warm golden glow and deep shadows, shocked and tragic mood. No signs, labels, writing or lettering anywhere in the image. Dark Fantasy Oil Painting, heavy brushwork.
