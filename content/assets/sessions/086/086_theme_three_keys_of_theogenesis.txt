A vast candlelit chamber carved deep into living rock beneath a temple, its walls covered with thousands of mirrors in gilded and silver frames, the floor a mosaic of blue and green stones, hundreds of candle flames multiplied endlessly in the glass. In the centre, on a heap of silk cushions, lies a great vain dragon gazing into a tall oval mirror. Instead of his own reflection, three neighbouring mirrors around him glow with three visions: in the first, a winged staff entwined with serpents lying on a stone bier inside a dark grass-covered burial mound sealed with glowing runes; in the second, a clay amphora overflowing with thick golden honey-like nectar inside a wooden fort deep in a pine forest; in the third, a pillar of white-gold primordial fire burning in a black abyss beneath the drowned ruins of an undersea city of sirens.

The dragon, lying curled on the cushions with his chin raised toward the mirror: adult male bronze dragon, noble proud build, polished golden-bronze scales, bright green eyes, crystal-like faceted bronze spikes on the head, shoulders and tail, glowing turquoise spiral rune markings on the forelegs and flank, large wings with deep blue inner membranes edged with gold wave-pattern filigree.

On a low pedestal beside the cushions, a winged siren with grey-green feathered wings sings without words, eyes fixed on the dragon.

Warm golden candlelight, cool turquoise glow from the visions in the mirrors, a sense of hidden ambition and secrets. Dark Fantasy Oil Painting, heavy brushwork, rich detail.
