A mythic moment of release on a barren islet in a dead sea. The sky is a starless void lit by a sourceless grey glow; black glassy water stretches to a horizon that does not exist. In the center of the composition a two-handed crystal scythe lies shattered on the bare rock, its translucent blade split lengthwise by a single axe blow, white light exploding outward from the fracture. From the broken crystal pour thousands upon thousands of tiny points of soul-light, rising into the void like sparks from a kicked bonfire, each a released human soul.

Standing over the shattered weapon, mid-follow-through with an enormous ancient bronze battleaxe still low from the swing, head lifted to watch the souls ascend, mouth open in a wordless cry: powerful male minotaur with a muscular build, broad shoulders, and brown fur. His bovine head features large, curved dark grey horns, piercing glowing blue eyes, and a bull snout. He has stylish, thick blonde hair swept back and a well-groomed blonde fur beard. He is dressed in a draped white toga with an ornate red sash featuring carved patterns and a textured grey shoulder strap. The aesthetic is a Greco-Roman gladiator and mythological creature.

Two of the rising soul-lights have stopped and hover close beside the minotaur's shoulder and brow, brighter and warmer than the rest, almost taking the suggestion of two human silhouettes. Behind and to the left, at the edge of the light, a darker, mossier sphere of energy crawls out of the scythe's remains and briefly resolves into a towering half-material humanoid silhouette, faceless, watching the scene without moving.

In the far background an unusual galleon rides at anchor with no sails and no oars, its hull glowing faintly from within where an elemental is bound.
