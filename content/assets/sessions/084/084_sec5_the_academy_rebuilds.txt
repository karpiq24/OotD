A bright, hopeful courtyard scene at a Greco-Roman school of magic. Cracked columns are being re-plastered; a huge crowd of new students in plain robes queues for enrollment at a long table, more than the clerks can handle. Above them, in the open sky over the courtyard, a fragile experimental flying machine of canvas, bronze fittings and articulated wooden wings banks awkwardly but successfully, trailing a thin plume of steam.

1. In an open workshop under the colonnade, surrounded by half-built wing frames, gears, drawings and failed prototypes: a very old male inventor, wiry and stooped, with a long wild white beard, deeply lined sun-browned face, thick brass-rimmed goggles pushed up on his forehead, wearing a stained leather apron over a simple grey tunic, delightedly explaining a hinge to a cluster of young students.

2. Standing at the workshop entrance with one hand raised in an approving gesture, having just arranged the whole thing: young adult male human wizard with a lean, rugged build. He has intense blue-grey eyes, a strong jawline, a weathered complexion, and a prominent scar across his right cheek. His short, messy dark brown hair is complemented by a full dark brown beard. He wears layered medieval clothing, a rust-brown hooded tunic over a coarse off-white linen shirt, with a thick brown leather shoulder strap and buckle. Two bronze circular geometric star emblems are visible on his chest.

Marble busts, scroll racks, chalked equations on a slate wall, and conspicuously empty lecterns where teachers who died on the walls should be standing.

High quality digital fantasy art, dramatic cinematic lighting, painterly detail. Warm ochres and clear blue sky, optimistic, full of small human detail.
