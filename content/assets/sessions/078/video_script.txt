## 1. Google Flow Project Assets Setup
Before generating the scenes, set up these character and location assets in your Google Flow project:

### Characters

#### @Arevon
- **Image Prompt**: young adult male elf with a slender build. He has an angular face with high cheekbones, a sharp jawline, intense dark eyes, and long pointed ears. His shoulder-length, wavy dark brown hair frames his face. He wears layered brown leather armor with intricate silver filigree details over a dark, high-collared tunic. He is equipped with black fingerless leather gloves, an ornate silver pendant with a large cracked dark gemstone, and a single silver diamond-shaped earring. The overall aesthetic is fantasy adventurer.
- **Character Personality**: Wise and inquisitive, seeking answers about the cosmos. He is deeply connected to nature and the stars, showing naive curiousness but growing into a strategic, stellar-form-shifting druid. Committed to his allies, even willing to sacrifice his legendary speed for their common goal.
- **Voice Characteristics**: Melodious, calm, and deliberate, speaking with a gentle but firm tone. He has a measured cadence, occasionally carrying an air of foreign elegance and cosmic wonder.

#### @Versir
- **Image Prompt**: adult male with a dark fantasy aesthetic. He has a slender build, pale alabaster skin, and short blonde hair styled in a messy quiff. His face is defined by a strong jawline, high cheekbones, and piercing cyan eyes, often held in a confident smirk. A large, jagged scar runs along the side of his neck and jaw. He wears an ornate breastplate. He wears a large, magical gauntlet on one hand.
- **Character Personality**: Enigmatic, charismatic, and intensely driven by a personal quest for vengeance. As a half-tytan, he exhibits a dark paladin's resolve, willing to make cold, ruthless tactical sacrifices for the greater good of Thylea. He hides a deeply strategic mind behind a confident, sometimes cynical smirk.
- **Voice Characteristics**: Smooth, low, and silver-tongued. He speaks with a smooth, commanding authority that can quickly turn chillingly cold, utilizing telepathic whispers or booming divine commands with equal ease.

#### @Felicjan
- **Image Prompt**: young adult male human wizard with a lean, rugged build. He has intense blue-grey eyes, a strong jawline, a weathered complexion, and a prominent scar across his right cheek. His short, messy dark brown hair is complemented by a full dark brown beard. He wears layered medieval clothing, a rust-brown hooded tunic over a coarse off-white linen shirt, with a thick brown leather shoulder strap and buckle. Two bronze circular geometric star emblems are visible on his chest.
- **Character Personality**: Brilliant but plagued by self-doubt and fear of failure, which he overcame to fulfill his destiny as "The Gifted One". He is highly intelligent, pragmatic, and possesses a sharp, witty sense of humor. He cares deeply for his wife Melania and is fiercely protective of his allies, showing immense tactical creativity in battle.
- **Voice Characteristics**: Warm, slightly gravelly, and quick-witted. He speaks with a rhythmic, academic clarity that shifts into intense, commanding incantations when wielding high-tier magic.

#### @Acastus
- **Image Prompt**: middle-aged male, muscular build, ancient Greek king, stern expression, dark intense eyes, furrowed brow, short dark hair, a thick black beard and mustache, wearing a golden laurel wreath crown, an ornate golden cuirass with a pauldron shaped like a snarling lion's head, a red cape over his shoulder.
- **Character Personality**: Popular with the common folk but secretly arrogant, impatient, and deeply insecure, suffering from an inferiority complex relative to his ancestor Estor Arkelander. Fearful of death, he is manipulative and power-hungry, though he briefly found a desperate resolve when urged by his mother to put his people first during the siege.
- **Voice Characteristics**: In his prime, he spoke with a booming, theatrical authority. Now, under stress, his voice is frayed, strained, and defensive, cracking with desperate pride and resentment.

#### @Idylla
- **Image Prompt**: elegant middle-aged human woman of royal stature. She has high cheekbones, calculating brown eyes, and long, braided dark brown hair woven with thin silver threads. She wears a draped and embroidered emerald-green chiton with delicate silver leaf patterns along the collar and hem, secured by silver fibulae on her shoulders. Her posture is poised and commanding.
- **Character Personality**: Politically brilliant, manipulative, and dignified. She has a sharp grasp of courtly intrigue and exercises a deep, maternal authority over her son Acastus. While she can be cold and self-serving, she is realistic and possesses the courage to face grim truths, urging her son to abandon his foolish pride when his city is on the brink of destruction.
- **Voice Characteristics**: Soft but remarkably clear and commanding. Her tone is melodic and carrying, utilizing a maternal warmth that can instantly turn into a sharp, icy whip of disapproval.

### Locations

#### @PalacKrolewski
- **Image Prompt**: massive ancient Greek palace interior, a grand throne room. The structure features high-vaulted ceilings, sweeping marble pillars, grand archways, and royal bronze plaques with legal codes. A decorative glass dome on the ceiling allows dim light to filter in. At the far end of the room stands a massive golden throne. The floor is polished marble, now covered in dust and debris from the ongoing siege outside. The air is thick with rising smoke and tension.
