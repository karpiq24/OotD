A fierce clash in the narrow, stone-paved streets of a burning ancient city. On one side, wild barbarians with indigo body paint, wielding heavy axes and round shields, charge forward. On the other side, towering brass and copper automaton soldiers of a storm god, with glowing blue cores and clockwork joints, strike back with heavy polearms. Buildings are engulfed in flames and embers fill the air. High action, dynamic motion, dramatic firelight, smoky environment, Arcane Studio Fortiche style.
