A ferocious mid-air pileup in an ancient colosseum. Two large bronze dragons dive from different angles onto a massive mutated silver dragon on the arena floor, biting and clawing at its twisted spiky hide. A third bronze dragon descends from above, mouth open in a wide cone of dragon breath washing over the mutant's back.

Bronze dragons: large and powerful with shimmering metallic bronze-copper scales, long necks, wide bat-like wings, sharp claws and horns, intelligent eyes — one biting at the mutant's neck, one raking its claws down the corrupted flank.

Mutated silver dragon below: monstrous corrupted body covered in jagged ice-like spikes, writhing in pain from the triple assault, malformed wings pinned, black corrupting aura flaring around the points of impact.

The stone gladiator colosseum rises around the scene — tiered marble seating, marble statues at the rim, the sky beyond torn by lightning. Dust and sand billow across the arena floor.
