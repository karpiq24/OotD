A cataclysmic aftershock following a god's death, seen across a ruined Greek-style coastal city. A sudden violent earthquake shakes the land, cracks splitting the streets, as a distant extinct volcano on a hill above the city awakens, belching thick black smoke and gouts of fire and glowing lava into a smoke-darkened sky.

In the middle distance, remnants of a monstrous army — flying minotaurs, cyclopes and giants — break into panicked flight, while in the streets fighting still rages and a last great sea monster churns the waters of the bay offshore. Rubble, broken columns, scattered fires, and dust hang over the devastated cityscape.

Ominous, apocalyptic atmosphere, orange volcanic glow against grey smoke. High quality digital fantasy art, epic scale, dramatic lighting, dramatic effects.
