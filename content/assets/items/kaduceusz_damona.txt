A legendary staff standing upright in a stone cleft inside a sealed burial mound, lit by a pale beam of light falling through a crack in the ceiling. The staff is a tall rod of dark aged bronze with green verdigris in its grooves; five slender serpentine dragons, each with its own distinct horned head, coil and twist around the shaft in a spiral, their scales finely engraved. The top of the staff is crowned by two great outstretched dragon wings of bronze, spread wide like a caduceus, with a faint pulse of white-gold life magic between them and a thin wisp of shadowy black death magic curling at the base. Around the cleft: ancient carved runes glowing faint blue on the stone walls, scattered bones, a broken burial urn, cobwebs. Cold silver and teal lighting contrasted with the warm glint of bronze. Mysterious, sacred, ominous atmosphere. Dark Fantasy Oil Painting, heavy brushwork, high detail, no text, no people.
