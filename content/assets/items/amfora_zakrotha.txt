A still-life of a sacred relic resting on a marble pedestal inside a torchlit chamber of an old timber-and-stone hill fort in a dark pine forest. The relic is a tall ancient Greek amphora with two curved handles, painted in the black-figure style of a lost golden age: bands of meander patterns, laurel garlands and small figures of winged dragons and robed figures offering cups to the sky, the paint worn and chipped at the rim. The amphora is unstoppered and thick, golden, honey-like nectar glistens at its mouth; a single slow drop runs down the painted side, glowing faintly with warm inner light. A simple bronze drinking cup stands beside it, its bottom wet with gold. Warm amber torchlight, dust motes in the air, deep shadows behind, a heavy fur pelt and a battered bronze-studded shield hanging on the rough wall in soft focus. Sense of divine, timeless power. Dark Fantasy Oil Painting, heavy brushwork, rich golden and umber palette, high detail, no text, no people.
