A primordial flame burning in the depths of a vast underwater abyss. Far below the surface of a dark sea, among the drowned ruins of a sunken marble city with broken colonnades and toppled statues covered in coral and kelp, a single column of living fire burns undimmed by the water: white-gold at its core, edged with deep orange and crimson, shaped like the first flame of creation. The water around it boils into rising silver bubbles, and its light throws long golden rays across the black seabed and the ruins, revealing ancient reliefs of a world being shaped by fire. High above, faint shafts of moonlight from the surface; schools of small fish scatter at the edge of the glow. Awe-inspiring, mythic, lonely atmosphere. Dark Fantasy Oil Painting, heavy brushwork, deep blue-black and blazing gold palette, high detail, no text, no people, no weapons.
