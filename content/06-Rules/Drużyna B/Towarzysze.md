## [[Astra]]

**Precognitive Vision (Reaction).** At the end of a long rest, Astra rolls two d20s and records the numbers. You can use your reaction to call on Astra to replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by you or a creature that you can see with one of these foretelling rolls. You must choose to do so before the roll is made. Once Astra has been called, she loses both foretelling rolls until she finishes a long rest.

## [[Kyrah]]

**Arkyrania's Wings (Reaction).** When an ally you can see is hit by an attack but before damage is rolled, fails a saving throw, or starts to fall, you can use your reaction to call on Kyrah. She swoops down in her dragon form and snatches the ally away. The ally takes no damage and suffers no other effects from the triggering attack, effect, or fall, and Kyrah carries them up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space of your choice. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

## [[Pythor]]

**Dragon's Thunderbolt (Reaction).** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can use your reaction to call on Pythor. A narrow bolt of lightning from the bronze dragon strikes the target. The attack deals an extra 4d6 lightning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
