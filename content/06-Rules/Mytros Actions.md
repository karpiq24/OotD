title: "Mytros Actions"
---


During their campaign, the heroes have gathered an impressive army of allies that will support them in the final clash for the fate of Thylea. In the most important encounters of the battle (Major Events), the party can call for aid using a mechanic similar to D&D Lair Actions.

## Identified Support
During the campaign, the party forged alliances with:
* **Dragons (The Former Five Gods)**: Mytros, Volkan, Pythor, Vallus, Kyrah, and newly hatched dragons: Paradox, Kairos, and the resurrected Nephele.
* **Flying Fortress and Ultros**: Ships commanded by Orestes, Daedalus, and Tars d'Lyrandar, offering artillery support.
* **Amazons of Themis**: Aerial griffon cavalry led by Queen Darien and Hippolyta.
* **Centaurs**: Various clans (including Hukar's centaurs).
* **Versi**: Daughter of Sydon, a powerful Oracle foreseeing the future.
* **Tribes of the Indigo Island**: Fierce barbarians unleashing their primal fury.

---

## Mytros Actions Mechanic

* **Initiative:** Actions are resolved on initiative count **10** (losing initiative ties, just like standard Lair Actions).
* **Choosing an Action:** When the initiative count is reached, the party as a whole (or a designated player/commander) decides which of the following options to use.
* **Restrictions:** Each action can only be used once per encounter. Using an action does not cost anyone's reaction or main action.

---

## Action List

### 1. Wrath of the Dragon Gods
The former Gods, shedding their humanoid guises, dive in their draconic forms over the battlefield.
* **Effect:** Choose a line that is 60 feet long and 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Additionally, choose one ally in that area – the flames bypass them, and the dragon's power restores 20 hit points to that ally.

### 2. Bombardment from the Fortress
Flying Fortress crew coordinates devastating drop of payloads and ballista bolts directly from the heavens.
* **Effect:** Choose a point you can see. A rain of projectiles falls in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point. Enemies in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. The area becomes difficult terrain.

### 3. Diving Amazon Griffons
Queen Darien and Hippolyta lead an aerial squadron of Amazons from the island of Themis.
* **Effect:** Target up to three creatures you can see. Each target becomes the victim of an immediate, ruthless charge: +10 to hit. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 + 4 slashing damage. Additionally, the strike disorients the enemy – until the end of its next turn, its speed is halved, and it can't take reactions.

### 4. Whispers of the Oracle
Versi reaches into the minds of the heroes, warning them of an impending blow.
* **Effect:** Choose up to three heroes. The next attack roll made against each of these heroes before initiative count 10 of the next round has disadvantage. Furthermore, each chosen hero gains 10 temporary hit points.

### 5. Trampling Centaur Hooves
Hukar's centaurs execute a devastating flanking charge, helping the heroes break out of encirclements.
* **Effect:** The horde stampedes across the battlefield in a line that is 40 feet long and 20 feet wide. Enemies in this area must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw. They take 4d10 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Each Hero within the area can use their reaction to immediately move up to half their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### 6. Primal Fury of the Indigo Tribes
The fierce barbarians of the Indigo Island unleash a ferocious battlecry that invigorates the heroes and shakes off debilitating effects.
* **Effect:** Choose up to three heroes. They gain advantage on their next saving throw before the end of the encounter. Additionally, they can automatically end one negative condition on themselves: paralyzed, incapacitated, blinded, deafened, frightened, grappled, restrained, or poisoned.
